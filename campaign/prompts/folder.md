# Folder, label and cursor prompts — Nano Banana 2.1

Pinks measured from reference A: back panel and tab `#CF5082`, front panel
`#F774AA`.

All three are generated on a flat mid-grey backdrop (`#8C8C8C`) so they cut out
cleanly: grey contrasts with the pink, with the cursor's black fill, and with
its white outline.

## Method

1. Generate the closed folder first (prompt 1). That is the master.
2. Split it into a back panel and a front panel by editing the master
   (prompts 2 and 3), so both halves keep the exact same style. The animation
   needs them as separate layers: the clothes come out between them and the
   front panel tilts open.
3. The label is best added as live text in the animation, so it can type
   itself out in the first second. Prompt 4 bakes it into the image, for a
   still or a reference.

## 1. Folder, closed (master)

```
A single macOS-style folder icon, front view, centred, isolated on a solid flat mid-grey background (#8C8C8C) with no gradient, no texture and no shadow on the background.

THE FOLDER:
- Modern macOS Big Sur style: a soft rounded rectangle with gently rounded corners, slightly wider than tall (about 5:4).
- Back panel in a deeper rose pink (#CF5082), with a small rounded tab rising from its top-left edge, the tab about one third of the folder's width.
- Front panel in a brighter candy pink (#F774AA), covering the lower part of the back panel and leaving a thin strip of the back panel visible above it, the front panel's top edge perfectly straight.
- Very subtle vertical gradient on each panel (slightly lighter at the top), a faint soft inner highlight along the front panel's top edge, and a very soft shadow where the front panel overlaps the back panel.
- Clean, smooth, flat-design surfaces: no paper texture, no grain, no reflections, no gloss, no 3D perspective.

COMPOSITION:
- Square 1:1 frame, the folder centred and filling about 70% of the frame width.
- Crisp, sharp edges suitable for cutting out.

DO NOT include: text, labels, logos, icons on the folder, papers sticking out, a cursor, a desktop, a window, or any other object.
```

```

Keep everything else exactly as in image 1: the back panel's size, shape, position in the frame, the tab, the colour, and the flat mid-grey background (#8C8C8C). Do not move, resize or restyle anything.

## 3. Front panel only (edit the master)

```

Replace everything that was the back panel with the flat mid-grey background (#8C8C8C). Keep the front panel's size, shape and position in the frame exactly as in image 1. Do not move, resize or restyle it.

## 5. Cursor

```
A single macOS arrow mouse pointer, isolated on a solid flat mid-grey background (#8C8C8C) with no gradient and no texture.

THE CURSOR:
- The standard macOS arrow: solid black fill, a clean white outline about one twelfth of the arrow's width, the tip pointing to the top-left.
- Classic proportions: a straight left edge, a diagonal right edge, and the short angled tail at the bottom.
- A very soft, small drop shadow down and to the right, as on macOS.
- Perfectly sharp vector-like edges, flat, no 3D, no gloss, no blur.

COMPOSITION:
- Square 1:1 frame. The cursor upright, not rotated, filling about 60% of the frame height, centred.

DO NOT include: a hand pointer, text, a folder, a desktop, a window, or any other object.
```
