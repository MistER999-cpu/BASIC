# Model figures for part 2 — Nano Banana 2.1

Six full-length editorial figures for the lookbook slide, generated one at a
time. All six share the CAMERA AND LIGHT block and the flat grey background so
they cut out cleanly and line up as one row in the meadow.

| # | Item | Colour | Model | Pose | Silhouette | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tank | Beige | A | S-curve contrapposto, arm behind head, thumb in waistband | Tall | Approved (file not yet received) |
| 2 | Tee set | White | B | Runway crossover step, mid-stride, hair lifting | Tall, wide | Approved (file not yet received) |
| 3 | Tank | Brown | A | Winged lean: hands on lower back, elbows out, leaning to camera | Medium | Approved (file not yet received) |
| 4 | Cami set | Beige | B | Wide lunge, hand on bent thigh, other hand in hair | Low, wide | Approved (file not yet received) |
| 5 | Cami set | White | A | Seated on the ground, leaning back on one hand, one knee up (replaces the crouch) | Lowest | Approved (file not yet received) |
| 6 | Tee set | Black | B | Finale stretch: wide stance, arms overhead, wrist held (alt: mid-air jump) | Tallest | Prompt ready |

Rules for the series:

- Garment always readable: no arms across the chest, no back views (no back
  references exist).
- Fixed scale: framed as if standing with head at ~6% and soles at ~92%; the
  camera never zooms for a pose, so a crouch really is smaller in the row.
- Camera at knee height, tilted slightly up; the meadow is generated to match.
- Barefoot, light breeze in the hair, calm unsmiling expression.
- Nano Banana copies poses from reference images: every prompt says which
  reference is for what and to ignore their poses (learned on figure 5).
