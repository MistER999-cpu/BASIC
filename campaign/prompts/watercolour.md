# Watercolour asset pack for part 2 — Nano Banana 2.1

The painted layer of the lookbook slide, in the style of reference B: rich
gouache-watercolour botanicals, oversized, composited over the photo meadow —
some in front of the models' legs, some towering behind them. Reference B had
three flowers for three models; this pack has one botanical per figure (each
tied to that figure's colourway), two fillers to carry the eye across the
longer pan, a butterfly in two wing positions so it can flap, and a sheet of
small drifting pieces.

Every prompt attaches `references/B-watercolour-style.jpg` as image 1, for
painting style only. Every element is painted on flat mid-grey (#8C8C8C) with
clean outer edges so it cuts out cleanly.
