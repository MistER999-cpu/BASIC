# Flat-lay prompts — Nano Banana 2.1

Six images: set 1 and set 2, each in white, beige and black. The tank
flat-lays already exist ([`../flatlays/tank/`](../flatlays/tank)) and are the
style every new image must match.

## Method

1. **One master per set, in beige.** Beige shows seams and stitching best;
   white burns out and black hides detail.
2. **White and black are recolours of the approved master**, not fresh
   generations, so shape and position stay identical across the three colours.
3. **Each set is one image**: top laid above the bottoms, its hem overlapping
   the waistband — one outfit, one file in the folder grid.

## Colours

Sampled from the photos. Whites and blacks follow the tank flat-lays so all
ten files sit together in the grid; each beige follows its own set.

| Colour | Hex | Source |
|---|---|---|
| White | `#F2EDF0` | white tank flat-lay — soft white, not pure white |
| Beige, set 1 | `#D8B5A2` | set 1 model photos — light nude |
| Beige, set 2 | `#B68F7C` | set 2 model photo — deeper mocha |
| Black | `#29272C` | black tank flat-lay — soft lifted black |
| Backdrop | `#BEBBC2` | tank flat-lays — cool light grey |

The two beiges are kept different because the photos differ. If they are
meant to be one dye, use one value for both.

## 1. Set 1 master — cami + bike shorts, beige

```
Create a professional e-commerce flat-lay photograph of the two-piece outfit worn in image 1, with the garments laid flat on their own — no person, no mannequin. Match the photographic style of image 2 exactly: same camera angle, backdrop, lighting, shadows and level of smoothing. Image 3 shows the same outfit on another body; use it only to confirm construction details.

THE GARMENTS (copy the construction from image 1 exactly):
- Top: a cropped camisole in smooth matte cotton-modal stretch jersey. Thin spaghetti straps about 1 cm wide. Soft, shallow V-shaped scoop neckline finished with a narrow self-fabric binding. Slim, straight body. Straight cropped hem finished with a twin-needle coverstitch. No lace, no padding seams, no hardware, no labels.
- Bottoms: high-rise bike shorts in the same jersey. Flat waistband about 5 cm deep with no drawstring and no visible elastic. A single centre-front seam running from the waistband down to the crotch. Mid-thigh length. Hems finished with a twin-needle coverstitch. No pockets, no side panels, no logos.
- Colour of both pieces: light nude beige, hex #D8B5A2, identical between top and bottoms.

LAYOUT:
- Shot from directly overhead, camera perfectly perpendicular to the surface, no perspective distortion.
- The cami lies front side up at the top, straps laid flat and symmetrical along the shoulder line, neckline centred.
- The shorts lie front side up directly below, legs straight down and slightly apart, centre-front seam on the vertical centre line.
- The cami's hem overlaps the shorts' waistband by about 3 cm, so the two read as one outfit.
- Both garments perfectly symmetrical about the vertical centre line, smoothed flat with only the soft natural ease of jersey — no creases, no folds, no wrinkles.
- Square 1:1 frame. The outfit is centred and fills about 90% of the frame height, with even margins left and right.

BACKDROP AND LIGHT (match image 2):
- Seamless matte cool light-grey paper backdrop, about #BEBBC2, with the same gentle falloff towards the corners.
- Soft, diffused overhead light. A very faint, soft contact shadow along the garment edges only.
- Photorealistic, sharp focus edge to edge, fabric texture and every line of stitching crisp and visible, true-to-life colour.

DO NOT include: a person, body parts, a mannequin, hangers, tags, labels, logos, text, props, extra pockets, drawstrings, lace, ruching, or any design detail not present in image 1.
```

## 3. White recolour (run once per set)

```
Edit image 1. Change only the colour of both garments to a soft white, hex #F2EDF0, matching the white tank in image 2 in tone and fabric sheen. It must read as soft white fabric, not pure #FFFFFF: keep the binding, seams, coverstitched hems and soft fabric shading clearly visible.

Keep everything else exactly as in image 1: the garment shapes and outlines, their position and size in the frame, every seam and stitch line, the layout and overlap, the backdrop, the lighting and the shadows. Do not move, resize, redraw or smooth anything. Only re-render the fabric's own highlights and shading so they look natural in white.
```

## Checklist before approving a master

- [ ] Straps / sleeves, neckline depth and binding match the model photo
- [ ] Shorts end at mid-thigh; leggings at mid-calf
- [ ] Centre-front seam on the bottoms, nothing else added
- [ ] Top and bottoms are the same colour
- [ ] Truly top-down, symmetrical, no creases
- [ ] Backdrop and light sit next to the tank flat-lays without a visible jump
- [ ] Nothing invented: no logos, labels, pockets, drawstrings

If the capri set comes out too small in the square (it is the tallest
outfit), switch the layout line to: "T-shirt on the left, leggings on the
right, side by side with a 4 cm gap, both top-aligned."
