## 0. Garment analysis

### Set 1: Cropped cami + biker shorts ("The Cami Set")

| Part | Details to preserve |
|---|---|
| Cami | Very thin spaghetti straps (~5 mm, same fabric). Shallow soft V-neck with a narrow self-fabric binding. Fitted, smooth double-layer front. Straight cropped hem that ends exactly at the shorts' waistband. |
| Biker shorts | High rise. Wide flat folded waistband (no drawstring, no logo). A single vertical centre-front seam. Mid-thigh length (~8" inseam). Twin-needle stitched hem. |
| Fabric | Soft matte cotton-elastane jersey. Opaque, no sheen. |

### Set 2: Fitted tee + capri leggings ("The Tee Set")

| Part | Details to preserve |
|---|---|
| Tee | Slim, body-skimming short-sleeve T-shirt. Slightly wide, lowered crew neck with a narrow self-fabric binding. Set-in sleeves ending at mid-upper-arm with a twin-needle hem. Straight twin-needle hem at the high hip, just meeting the leggings' waistband. |
| Capri leggings | High rise. Wide flat waistband. Second-skin fit. Inseam visible. Ends at mid-calf with a clean twin-needle hem. No pockets, no logo. |
| Fabric | The same matte cotton-elastane jersey, slightly denser. Fully opaque. |

### Colors

| | Set 1 | Set 2 | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | Deep matte jet black | Same | Seams vanish, so always add a rim/edge light |
| White | Clean optic white | Same | AI makes it see-through or blown out, so say "fully opaque" and keep exposure slightly low |
| Beige | **Warm sand**: light, peachy | **Latte**: a touch deeper, more caramel-taupe | Merges with skin. Never write "nude"; always mention the visible seams, binding and hems |

> If both beiges are the same dye in real life, use the Set 1 line for both. The
> photos read slightly different, so the pack treats them as *sand* and *latte*.

### Model (keep identical everywhere)

`a young woman in her early 20s with warm light-olive skin, dark brown curly chin-length bob with a centre part, brown eyes, natural full brows, minimal dewy makeup, soft closed-lip smile`

## 1. Workflow (do this every time)

1. **Nano Banana 2.1:** attach the real product photo **in the color you are generating**.
   Never ask it to recolor a different color's photo. Also attach your best face shot
   as a second reference so the face stays consistent.
2. Paste the prompt. Generate 4 variations. Keep the one where **seams, binding and hems match** the product photo.
3. **OmniFlash:** use that still as the **start frame** (image-to-video) and paste the matching video prompt.
4. Default ratio is **9:16** (Reels/TikTok/Stories). For a feed post, change it to **4:5**. For a website hero, use **16:9**.
5. If a tool has a seed or a "character" lock, reuse the same seed per set.

## 8. Consistency tips

- **Always attach the real photo of the exact color** you're generating, plus your best face reference.
- **Name the seams.** The details that make the garment look real are "centre-front seam", "self-fabric binding" and "twin-needle hem". Keep them in every prompt.
- **Black:** always ask for a rim or edge light. **White:** always say "fully opaque" and keep the exposure balanced. **Beige:** always say it is "clearly distinct from her skin". Avoid the word "nude".
- **Don't over-move the camera** in OmniFlash. Slow push-ins, dollies and arcs keep the garment stable. Fast moves cause morphing.
- **Back views:** I can only describe the front from these photos. Send back views if you want back or 360° shots.
- **Generate several takes, then pick one.** Reject any frame where a strap, hem or seam changes. Those are the first things an AI drifts on.
