# survêt: Nano Banana Pro shot prompts

## How many pictures

| | Per colour | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Action shots (prompts 1–7) | 7 | 21 |
| Detail close-ups (prompts 8–9) | 2 | 6 |
| End card (prompt 10) | 1 | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** | **30** |

The edit has about 30 strip slots per colour. With 9 shots per colour, each one
comes back about 3 times, with a different crop each time, so nothing feels
repeated. **Minimum:** 6 per colour (prompts 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8) plus the end card.

Use the **same 10 prompts for all three colours**. When the colours mix at the
end, the same pose then shows up side by side in sand, black and brown, which
looks deliberate.

## Settings (every image)

- **Aspect ratio: 9:16** (vertical)
- **Resolution: 2K.** The edit zooms in up to 2.3×, and 1K goes soft. 4K isn't
  needed.
- **Reference images:** for each colour, attach that colour's front + back product
  photo (the two-panel image you sent me).

## Workflow: one chat per colour

1. Open a **new chat** for each colour, so the colours don't bleed into each other.
2. Attach that colour's product photo and send the **setup prompt** below, with
   the colour filled in.
3. Then send prompts **1 → 10** one at a time in the **same chat**. Nano Banana Pro
   remembers the model and outfit from the setup, so the shot prompts don't need
   to repeat them.
4. If an image drifts (different face, wrong colour, extra pockets, a logo
   appears), use the fix-up prompts at the bottom instead of starting over.

## Setup prompt (send first, once per colour)

Replace `[COLOUR]` with **sand beige**, **charcoal black** or **chocolate brown**.

```
I'm shooting a fashion campaign for a tracksuit. The attached image is the product
reference: front and back of the same model wearing it. For every image I ask for
in this chat, keep these exactly the same:

MODEL: the same young woman as in the reference, with an identical face, the same
curly, shoulder-length, dark brown hair and the same natural makeup. Do not
change her face, age or body shape.

OUTFIT: [COLOUR] two-piece made of soft, matte, fine-knit jersey:
- an oversized long-sleeve top with a ribbed crew neck, dropped shoulder seams,
  long wide sleeves that reach the knuckles, a hip-length hem with side slits,
  and the back slightly longer than the front
- matching straight, wide-leg trousers with an elastic waist hidden under the
  top, falling in a soft break over the shoes
- the same low-profile retro suede trainers with gum soles as in the reference
The colour must match the reference exactly: [COLOUR], the same shade on the top,
the trousers and in every image. No logos, no prints, no pockets, no zips, no
drawstrings.

SET: a seamless light-grey studio backdrop (#F1F0F3) with a soft floor curve,
large soft key light from the front-left, gentle fill, soft natural shadows.
Clean, high-end streetwear editorial look, true-to-life fabric texture, sharp
focus.

FORMAT: vertical 9:16, 2K. Keep her centred left-to-right. No text, no
watermark, no other people.

Reply "ready" and wait for my shot prompts.
```

## Shot prompts (send one at a time)

### 1 · Worm's-eye hero
```
Shot 1. Camera on the studio floor between her feet, 14mm ultra-wide lens,
pointing almost straight up at her. She stands tall with her feet apart and looks
down into the lens with a calm, confident expression. Her trainers and the trouser
hems are huge and close in the foreground, and her body tapers up to her face
against the light-grey ceiling of the backdrop. Strong perspective, everything
sharp from shoes to face.
```

### 2 · Top-down
```
Shot 2. Bird's-eye view from directly overhead, the camera 3 metres above her
pointing straight down, 24mm lens. She stands on the light-grey studio floor,
tilts her head all the way back and looks straight up into the lens. Her arms
hang loose, and the wide sleeves and the tunic's shoulders frame her face. The
trousers and trainers are foreshortened below her, and she casts a soft shadow
on the floor.
```

### 3 · Walk-in
```
Shot 3. She walks fast straight toward the camera, caught mid-stride with her
front heel just touching down. The side slit of the top flares open with the
step, the wide trouser legs swing, and her sleeves move with her arms.
Camera at hip height, 35mm lens, full body. Slight motion blur on the trouser
hems and sleeve ends only, face pin-sharp, looking straight into the lens.
```

### 4 · Jump
```
Shot 4. She is caught at the very top of a jump, both feet tucked up behind her,
the soles of her trainers visible, about 50cm off the floor. Her arms are lifted
loosely at her sides, the sleeves and the hem of the top float up, and her curls
bounce. Camera low at knee height, angled slightly up, 24mm lens, full body
with space below her feet. Frozen motion, a joyful, open-mouthed laugh.
```

### 5 · Over the shoulder
```
Shot 5. Her back is to the camera, showing the longer back hem and the dropped
shoulder seams. She twists her upper body and turns her head over her left
shoulder to look straight into the lens with a slight smirk. Her left hand lightly
pulls the right sleeve down over her fingers. Camera at shoulder height, 50mm
lens, framed from head to mid-calf.
```

### 6 · Dutch tilt lean
```
Shot 6. Dutch angle: the whole camera is rolled 20 degrees clockwise. She leans
back with her weight on one leg, the other leg crossed in front at the ankle,
both hands tucked inside the ends of the long sleeves and her arms loosely folded.
Her head tilts the opposite way to the camera, with a cool, unimpressed
expression. Camera at chest height, 35mm lens, full body.
```

### 7 · Fisheye crouch
```
Shot 7. She drops into a low streetwear squat on her heels, knees wide,
forearms resting on her knees, one hand reaching toward the lens with the sleeve
slipping back. Fisheye lens about 30cm from her hand, at her eye level, so the hand
and knees are exaggerated and big and her face is close behind. The wide trousers
pool around the trainers. Playful, direct eye contact, the curved fisheye
distortion clearly visible.
```

### 8 · Detail: neckline + sleeve
```
Shot 8. Extreme close-up detail, 100mm macro lens. Framed from her lips down to
mid-chest: the ribbed crew neck, the dropped shoulder seam and one long sleeve
cuff falling over her hand, which rests on her collarbone. The fine-knit jersey
texture is visible, with soft side light that shows the rib. Shallow depth of
field, crisp focus on the neckline.
```

### 9 · Detail: hem + trainers
```
Shot 9. Ground-level close-up, camera on the floor, 50mm lens. Framed from the
hip-length hem of the top down to the floor: the side slit opening, the wide
trouser legs breaking over the trainers, and one foot stepping forward with
the gum sole toward the camera. Soft shadow on the light-grey floor, crisp focus
on the trainer and the trouser hem.
```

### 10 · End card
```
Shot 10. A calm, classic full-length front view: she stands straight and relaxed,
arms by her sides, a soft smile into the lens. Camera at waist height, 70mm lens,
straight on, no tilt. The whole body is visible from the top of her hair to the
trainers, with generous empty light-grey space above her head (about a quarter
of the frame) and a little space below her feet. Perfectly even lighting.
```

## Fix-up prompts (when an image drifts)

- **Face changed:** `Same image, but restore her face to exactly match the
  reference: same features, same curly hair. Change nothing else.`
- **Colour off:** `Same image, but correct the outfit colour to exactly match the
  [COLOUR] in the reference, the same on the top and trousers. Change nothing
  else.`
- **Garment details wrong:** `Same image, but remove any pockets, logos, zips or
  drawstrings. The top must have a plain ribbed crew neck and side slits, like the
  reference.`
- **Background drifted:** `Same image, but put her back on the plain seamless
  light-grey studio backdrop, with no props.`
- **Cropped body:** `Same shot, zoom out slightly so her whole body including
  the trainers fits in the frame.`

## Naming and sending

Name files `colour-number`, e.g. `sand-01.jpg` … `sand-09.jpg`, and name the end
card `sand-end.jpg`. Send them all over (a zip is fine). They go into
`survet/shots/sand|black|brown/`. The `end` in the name is how the edit finds the
end card.
